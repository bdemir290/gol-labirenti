# Goal Maze - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · [Türkçe](gizlilik.html)

Goal Maze (Gol Labirenti) is a single-player puzzle game. We do not collect or sell your personal data. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob; see "Ads" below.

## Stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, streak, chosen ball and pitch, sound and haptics settings) is stored only on your device.

## iCloud

If iCloud is on, your progress syncs between your own devices through Apple's iCloud. This data stays in your iCloud account; we cannot access it.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Ads

There are no forced ads. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one for a reward (coins, a life or extra kicks).

These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent abuse, AdMob may process device identifiers (the advertising identifier / IDFA, if you allow it), approximate location (from your IP address), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Details: [How Google uses data from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

The app asks for tracking permission on first launch. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", non-personalised ads are shown. Users in the EEA and the UK can change their ad consent at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from any age group.
